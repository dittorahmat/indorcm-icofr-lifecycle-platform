## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Scoping Grid Column Ordering
The system SHALL position the Lini 1 Owner (`departmentOwner`) and Owner Acceptance (`ownerAcceptance`) columns at the far right of the scoping General Ledger Data Grid.

#### Scenario: Visual column ordering
- **WHEN** the scoping General Ledger Data Grid is rendered
- **THEN** the Lini 1 Owner and Owner Acceptance columns SHALL be displayed to the right of the Application Significant column
