## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: CSA Assessment Dashboard
The system SHALL provide a comprehensive dashboard for CSA results. The Assessment History list SHALL be positioned on the **LEFT** side of the view, and the Assessment Chart/Flow Visualization SHALL be positioned on the **RIGHT** side.

#### Scenario: Layout arrangement
- **WHEN** user views the CSA Assessment tab
- **THEN** the History section MUST appear to the left of the Chart/Flow section.
