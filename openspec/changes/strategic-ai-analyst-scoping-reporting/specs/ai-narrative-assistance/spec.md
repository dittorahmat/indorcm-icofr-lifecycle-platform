## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Generative Narrative Assistance
The system SHALL provide AI-powered assistance for drafting complex regulatory justifications.

#### Scenario: Drafting Ineffective rationale
- **WHEN** user asks "Bantu saya tulis rationale kenapa kontrol ini Ineffective"
- **THEN** the AI SHALL retrieve the test findings and deficiency details
- **AND** generate a professional draft narrative formatted for management assessment.

#### Scenario: Sampling Homogeneity Justification
- **WHEN** user asks "Bantu buatkan justifikasi homogenitas untuk sampling TOE"
- **THEN** the AI SHALL provide a draft based on the control's nature and frequency attributes.
