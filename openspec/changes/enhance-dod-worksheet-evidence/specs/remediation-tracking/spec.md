## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: DoD Worksheet Integration into Remediation Workflow
The system SHALL bind the severity level, box rationale breakdown, and evidence references generated by the DoD Worksheet to the deficiency record and trigger the corresponding governance reporting route (Tabel 24 SK-5/2024).

#### Scenario: Material Weakness notification routing
- **WHEN** the DoD Worksheet concludes with 'Material Weakness'
- **THEN** the system SHALL mark the deficiency with Material Weakness severity and designate reporting requirement to CEO, Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, and Komite Audit
